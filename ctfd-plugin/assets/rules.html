Competition Rules

Read carefully before competing. Violations may result in disqualification.

01 General

  • By participating you agree to abide by all rules listed here and any additional rules announced by the organizers.
  • The organizers reserve the right to disqualify any team or individual at their sole discretion.
  • Rules may be updated during the competition. Changes will be announced on the main channel.

02 Teams

  • Teams are pre-registered by the organizers. No self-registration is allowed.
  • Each team member must use the account assigned to them. Sharing credentials between teams is prohibited.
  • Collaboration between teams is strictly forbidden.

03 Flags & Scoring

  • Flags are unique per team. Submitting another team's flag is detected as cheating and will be logged.
  • Flag format: infodays{...}
  • Flags are case-sensitive. Copy them exactly as found.
  • Each challenge can only be solved once per team. No partial scoring.
  • The scoreboard is the single source of truth. In case of a tie, the team that reached the score first wins.

04 Challenge Instances

  • Some challenges spawn a dedicated instance for your team. Use the Start/Stop buttons in the challenge modal.
  • Each team can run a limited number of instances simultaneously. Stop instances you no longer need.
  • Instances automatically expire after a set time. You can restart them if needed.
  • Do not attempt to access another team's challenge instance. Access is monitored and restricted.

05 Fair Play

  • Do not attack the CTF infrastructure (scoreboard, challenge servers, network). Only attack what the challenge asks you to attack.
  • Do not perform denial-of-service attacks against any system.
  • Do not share flags, hints, or solutions with other teams.
  • Do not use brute force against the flag submission endpoint.
  • All cheating attempts are automatically logged and reviewed.

06 Communication

  • Official announcements will be posted on this platform. Check regularly.
  • If you believe a challenge is broken, contact the organizers. Do not post about it publicly.
  • Writeups may be published after the competition ends, not during.

07 Disclaimer

Techniques learned here are for educational purposes only. Applying them against systems without explicit authorization is illegal. The organizers are not responsible for any misuse of knowledge gained during this competition.

Good luck, have fun, and hack responsibly.
